Questions

Real estate questions. Straight answers.

Can it take leads from listing portals?

Often, yes, through the lead notification email or a direct connection, depending on the portal and your account. We check your sources on the call and write exactly what is possible into the scope.

I already have a real estate CRM. Do I have to switch?

No. We build on your existing CRM if it can do the job. If it cannot, we say so on the call and the move is part of the price, not a surprise.

Will it text leads without me knowing?

Only the messages you approve in the scope, and only to people who opted in. Every message honors STOP. You review the wording so it fits your brokerage’s rules.

Does it work for a team?

Yes. New leads can go to one agent or be shared across a team, with the booking on the right calendar. We map who gets what in the scope.

Do you promise more closings?

No. We promise scope and schedule, not results. You get a working system you can test from your own phone: a lead gets the text and email, and a call lands on your calendar.
