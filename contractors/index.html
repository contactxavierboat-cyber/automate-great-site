Questions

Contractor questions. Straight answers.

Which trades is this for?

Any trade that wins work from estimate requests: roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, remodeling, pools, landscaping, painting and similar. If your jobs start with a call or a form, it fits.

I already use a field service app. Do I have to switch?

No. We build on what you have if it can do the job. If it cannot, we say so on the call and the move is part of the price, not a surprise.

Will it text my customers without me knowing?

Only the messages you approve in the scope, and only to people who opted in on your form. Every message honors STOP.

Do you run my ads?

We set up the ad account, install the pixel and make the first creative. Ads only turn on after the funnel works, and the spend stays in your own account.

Do you promise more jobs?

No. We promise scope and schedule, not results. You get a working system you can test from your own phone.
