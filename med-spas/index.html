Questions

Med spa questions. Straight answers.

Do you handle patient records or health information?

No. We work on the scheduling side only: name, contact details, the service someone asked about and the appointment time. Charts, intake answers, treatment notes and photos stay in the system you already use. We do not handle medical records or card data beyond standard checkout.

Do I have to switch booking software?

No. We build on what you have if it can do the job. If it cannot, we say so on the call and the move is part of the price, not a surprise.

Will it text my clients without me knowing?

Only the messages you approve in the scope, and only to people who opted in on your form. Messages stay about times and logistics, never treatment details. Every message honors STOP.

Do you promise fewer no-shows or more bookings?

No. We promise scope and schedule, not results. You get a working system you can test from your own phone: a request gets the text and email, and a booking lands on your calendar with its reminders.

Do you run my ads?

We set up the ad account, install the pixel and make the first creative. Ads only turn on after the booking page works, and the spend stays in your own account.
